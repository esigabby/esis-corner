About Esi

Gabrielle Blake • Esi Love • Practitioner & Organizer

Hey! My name is Esi Love (Gabrielle "Gabby" Blake). I am a spiritual practitioner, poet, domestic violence survivor, and community activist with a thriving TikTok community of over 25,000+ followers and a dedicated sanctuary on X (Twitter).

My spiritual journey began in 2019, and I started sharing my gifts through readings in 2020. Hoodoo & Vodou are the foundational roots of my practice, and I have cultivated an incredibly strong bond with the Ancestors and the Dead. When it comes to my readings, I primarily work with cartomancy (playing cards) and oracle decks to deliver honest, transformative, and deeply grounded messages.

Frontline Organizing (2017 – Present)

Activism, Movement Leadership & Press

📰 Featured in The Associated Press & Huffington Post →

My organizing roots started back in Howland & the Mahoning Valley, Ohio in 2017, marching in the Nonviolence Rallies and organizing youth initiatives to stand up against systemic injustice.

Transitioning to Kent State University, I served as both President and Director of Political Affairs & Grievances for Black United Students (BUS), leading the campus through historic demonstrations, campus safety advocacy, and student power reckonings.

✊🏾 Black Liberation & Kent State Protests

Mobilized on the frontlines of the 2020 George Floyd uprisings and led the historic Kent State Rock protests against racist campus vandalism to demand systemic accountability and protection for Black students.
